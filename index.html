10 BBQ spots worth the drive

A glimpse of the smokehouses BBQ fans plan whole trips around. These are our editorial picks drawn from well-known national lists, not an official ranking.

Goldee's Barbecue

Fort Worth, TX

Texas brisket

Texas Monthly's No. 1 pick in its 2021 ranking and a Michelin Bib Gourmand. Open Friday to Sunday for lunch, until it sells out.

4645 Dick Price Rd, Fort Worth, TX 76140

See on PitQuest

Franklin Barbecue

Austin, TX

Central Texas

The brisket line that launched a thousand road trips. Open Tuesday to Sunday from 11 a.m. until sold out. Bring a chair.

900 E 11th St, Austin, TX 78702

See on PitQuest

Joe's Kansas City Bar-B-Que

Kansas City, KS

Kansas City

Famous ribs and burnt ends, served from inside a working gas station since 1996. Closed Sundays.

3002 W 47th Ave, Kansas City, KS 66103

See on PitQuest

Central BBQ (Midtown)

Memphis, TN

Memphis ribs

Dry-rubbed ribs and pulled pork from the original Midtown spot, rebuilt and reopened in January 2025.

Central Ave, Memphis, TN 38104

See on PitQuest

Scott's Bar-B-Que

Hemingway, SC

Whole hog

The family smokehouse where pitmaster Rodney Scott learned whole hog, cooked over wood with a vinegar-based sauce. Limited hours, so check before you go.

2734 Hemingway Hwy, Hemingway, SC 29554

See on PitQuest

Bogart's Smokehouse

St. Louis, MO

St. Louis style

A Soulard favorite known for its blowtorch-finished ribs and a regular on national must-visit lists.

1627 S 9th St, St. Louis, MO 63104

See on PitQuest

Moo's Craft Barbecue

Los Angeles, CA

Texas style, global twist

Texas-style smoke with Asian and Latin influences, and a Michelin Bib Gourmand five years running.

2118 N Broadway, Los Angeles, CA 90031

See on PitQuest

Heritage Barbecue

San Juan Capistrano, CA

California brisket

Central Texas-style barbecue next to Mission San Juan Capistrano, with a Michelin Bib Gourmand.

31721 Camino Capistrano, San Juan Capistrano, CA 92675

See on PitQuest

Little Miss BBQ (University)

Phoenix, AZ

Central Texas style

Ranked No. 1 on Yelp's 2026 Top 100 BBQ restaurants in the U.S. Known for fatty brisket, usually sold out by early afternoon.

4301 E University Dr, Phoenix, AZ 85034

See on PitQuest

Big Bob Gibson Bar-B-Q

Decatur, AL

Alabama white sauce

Serving barbecue since 1925 and the birthplace of Alabama white sauce. Try it on the smoked chicken.

1715 6th Ave SE, Decatur, AL 35601

See on PitQuest

Kirbee's BBQ

Greenpoint, Brooklyn, NY

Texas style, New York

From two Texas Monthly Top 50 pitmasters, Jonny White (formerly of Goldee's) and Chuck Charnichart (Barbs B Q). Open Friday to Sunday, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., and the line is part of the deal.

McGuinness Blvd, Greenpoint, Brooklyn, NY

See on PitQuest
New: opened March 2025

Elliott's BBQ Lounge

Florence, SC

Whole hog

Pee Dee whole hog cooked over pecan wood inside Seminar Brewing. Southern Living named it the best new BBQ joint in the South for 2026.

551 W Lucas St, Florence, SC 29501

See on PitQuest
New: opened Dec. 2025

Lewis Barbecue

Atlanta, GA

Texas style

Opened in Atlanta on December 8, 2025, and drew strong early reviews for its brisket.

Atlanta, GA (check the restaurant's site for the address)

Bucán Rodeo

San Antonio, TX

Food truck, Puerto Rican BBQ

Mesquite-smoked meats worked into pernil, arroz con pollo, and ropa vieja, served with mofongo and tostones. Recently settled at the Broadway News food truck park.
